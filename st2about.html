Philippines Travel Guide

ABOUT THE COUNTRY

The country has more than 7,600 islands in Southeast Asia, and is called an archipelago, divided into

three main regions: Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao. Known for the richness and the biodiversity, it sits

on the Pacific Ring of Fire, near the equator, and they also do not experience Winter or Fall - only the

rainy and hot weather, which explains their resilience as it can get to extreme temperatures.

CULTURE

A dynamic blend of indigenous Malay traditions and its centuries of Spanish, American, and Chinese influences according to the history.

The fusion features catholic faith, historic architecture, and diverse culinary traditions. Some Filipino words are derived from the Spaniards' language

as evidence. It also features the warm hospitality, the deep respect for elders with the terms 'po' and 'opo', as well as the emphasis of

bayanihan, which shows helping others and showing unity in their community. With over 170 languages across it's islands, the nation celebrates immense

regional diversity while uniting through the official languages of 'Filipino' and 'English', and the traditional folk stories and dances like Tinikling to

modern pop culture, the country remains a resilient, creative, and deeply expressive one.
